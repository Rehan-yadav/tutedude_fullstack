Libraries are the last truly public rooms in town.

Since the day we opened, Greenwood has been a place where anyone can sit down, open a book and stay as long as they like. No tickets, no pressure, just space to think.

Over the years the collection has grown to more than twelve thousand titles, and the building now holds reading halls, a children's corner, study rooms and a small archive of local history.

We host talks, workshops and weekend clubs, and we keep adding digital resources so that the library fits into modern routines without losing its calm.

Whether you come for a quiet hour, a research project or a community event, there is a seat waiting for you.
